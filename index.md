<section class="cv-section" id="biography">
  <h2 class="cv-section__title">Biography</h2>
  <p>
    I am currently a <strong>Postdoctoral Research Associate (PDRA)</strong> in the CoSTAR Real-time Lab
    (School of Informatics) at the <strong>University of Edinburgh</strong>, UK, working with
    <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=pKV2QvsAAAAJ&hl=en">Prof. Mohan Sridharan</a> and
    <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=cUYzSgYAAAAJ&hl=en">Dr. Eleonora D'Arnese</a>.
    My work spans computer vision, low-level vision, generative models, and 3D content generation, with current
    projects on 3D human motion style transfer and 3D asset generation.
    You can find my CV <a href="{{ "/assets/cv.pdf" | absolute_url }}">here</a>.
  </p>
  <p>
    I received my <strong>Ph.D. in Computer Science and Technology</strong> from the Harbin Institute of
    Technology, Shenzhen (HIT) in 2023, where I was fortunate to be co-supervised by
    <a href="https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=fhwB7UwAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN">Prof. Guangming Lu</a> and
    <a href="https://wenjiepei.github.io/">Prof. Wenjie Pei</a>. Before that, I earned my M.Eng. in Control
    Engineering from Beijing Forestry University, advised by Prof. Jiangming Kan.
  </p>
  <p>
    I previously served as a Technical Section Chief at the Information Center of China Tobacco, leading
    a core technical team on industrial AI applications including large language models, anomaly detection, and
    video understanding. I have also been a research intern at Huawei Noah's Ark Lab (low-level vision, mentored
    by <a href="https://scholar.google.com.hk/citations?user=0ua28KoAAAAJ&hl=zh-CN">Dr. Xueyi Zou</a>), at iQIYI
    (video understanding, mentored by Dr. Ruijin Jin), and a visiting intern at Peking University.
  </p>
  <p class="bio-contact">
    Email: <a href="mailto:fengx_hit@outlook.com">fengx_hit@outlook.com</a>
  </p>
</section>
